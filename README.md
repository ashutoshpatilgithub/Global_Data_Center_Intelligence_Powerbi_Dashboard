# Global Data Center Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

Data centers power the digital world, but they also consume huge amounts of electricity and produce significant carbon emissions. This project analyzes ~9,000 data center facilities across North America, Asia and Europe (US, Canada, France, India, Japan) from 2021 to 2025.


  Headline KPI	            Value
Total Facilities	           9K
Total Capacity (MW)	        376K
Average Utilization	       76.23 %
Total Electricity Use	     4.15 B
Total Carbon Emission	      674 M
Total Downtime Hours	     406.82 K

📌 What I Did
Cleaned and validated the data in Power Query (data types, nulls, duplicates, standardized text).
Created KPIs using DAX: facilities, capacity, utilization %, electricity use, carbon emission, downtime hours.
Built an interactive dashboard with slicers (Year, Region, Country, Cooling System, Owner, Status), charts and drill-down.

💡 Top Insights
🇺🇸 The US has the most capacity (129K MW); Enterprise sites are 55% of all facilities.
❄️ Air-cooled sites use the most electricity (1.83bn), so liquid cooling is the biggest efficiency opportunity.
⭐ Only 0.9K facilities have an "A" rating; B-rated sites have the most downtime (192.83K hrs).
💧 About 1 in 3 sites is in a high water-stress area.
⚡ Electricity usage spiked in 2025 and should be investigated.

✅ Recommendations
Upgrade air-cooled sites to liquid or more efficient cooling.
Move B and C rated facilities toward A to reduce downtime.
Increase renewable energy in high-emission cities.
Plan water-efficient cooling for high water-stress sites.
